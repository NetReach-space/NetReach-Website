Our Core Values

Open Source for the General Public

Open-source shouldn't mean hard-to-use. NetReach builds software that feels modern, slick, and ready out of the box for everyone.

User-Grade Simplicity

No complex setup scripts or terminal knowledge required. Simple touch interfaces designed for everyday mobile users.

Developer-Grade Tools

Power applications with detailed configurations, logging, local APIs, and deep customization for developers and power users.

Zero Tracking

Your personal data belongs to you. No background telemetry, no forced accounts, and no ad networks.

Transparent Source

Every application codebase is publicly available on GitHub under permissive open source licenses for peer review.
